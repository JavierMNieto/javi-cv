Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science

University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Ling Ren. My research interests lie in distributed algorithms, decentralized systems, and applied cryptography.

In summer 2026, I was a research intern at Category Labs. Before my Ph.D., I received a B.S. in Statistics & Computer Science from UIUC and worked at Amazon Robotics, AASI, and DecisionQ. I am a Sloan Scholar and have received the SURGE Fellowship, the Graduate College Fellowship, and an NSF GRFP Honorable Mention.
